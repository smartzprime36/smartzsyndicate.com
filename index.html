Stay safe

⚠️ Anti-scam rules

  • Admins never DM you first. Anyone who does is an impostor.
  • Never share your seed phrase or private key with anyone, for any reason. No admin, bot or "support" will ever ask for it.
  • Only trust the addresses on this page. Ignore addresses posted in DMs, replies or screenshots.
  • No one can "validate", "sync" or "unlock" your wallet. Those links drain wallets.

Syndicate Credits

Syndicate Credits are the in-app points used inside SmartzOS for games, missions and the shop. They are not a token, have no cash value and can't be redeemed or withdrawn.
